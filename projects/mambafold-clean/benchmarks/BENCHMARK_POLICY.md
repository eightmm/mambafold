# Benchmark admission policy

This policy separates stable evaluation inputs from claims about
generalization. It governs every number reported from this project root. It
does not govern the previous ESMC-6B track, whose corpus and claims live in the
original repository and may not be mixed with these.

Freeze every rule here before looking at model scores. Do not tune a rule to
improve a result.

## Claim boundary

A target may be called **coordinate-training-clean under this audit** only if:

1. its PDB or UniProt identity is absent from every coordinate-training source;
2. its canonical amino-acid sequence has no exact match in any source; and
3. it passes the predeclared MMseqs2 homology screen against the union of the
   admitted RCSB corpus and the AFDB SwissProt v4 corpus.

For the full-chain gate, exclude a target when MMseqs2 finds at least 30%
sequence identity over at least 80% of the benchmark-query sequence. Do not
require reciprocal coverage of the training sequence: training uses contiguous
random crops, so a target embedded in a longer training chain is relevant. Also
search each official CASP evaluation-unit sequence and exclude its parent
target when a hit reaches 30% identity over 80% of that domain query. For a
future set without official domains, freeze a domain segmentation before
prediction. Record the MMseqs2 version, database hashes, commands, query and
training-sequence coverage, identity, and excluded ids.

Passing these gates does **not** establish that a target was unseen by ESMC-6B.
ESMC is trained on large sequence collections whose complete per-sequence
membership is not available here, and the same caveat applies to SimpleFold's
ESM2-3B. Allowed wording is therefore "coordinate-training-clean under the
declared RCSB/AFDB audit," never "training-clean," "unseen," or "leakage-free."

## What the date rule already guarantees

RCSB coordinates are admitted only when released on or before 2020-05-01, so no
CASP14, CASP15, or CASP16 sequence matches the experimental half of the corpus.
The AFDB distillation stream is v4, generated by AlphaFold 2, whose 2018-04-30
training cutoff also precedes those targets — but its *sequences* are not date
filtered, here or in SimpleFold, and SwissProt covers UniProt. Three targets are
in the corpus verbatim:

| Benchmark | Excluded target | Matching training record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CASP15 | `T1106s2` | `AF-P0C2N2-F1-model_v4`, `AF-P61417-F1-model_v4` |
| CASP16 | `T1227s1` | `AF-P0AD04-F1-model_v4` |
| CASP16 | `T1243` | `AF-P0CH91-F1-model_v4`, `AF-Q7U1K0-F1-model_v4` |

A gate run against the experimental half alone would have reported all three
clean, which is why the gate takes the union of both training sources.

`T1045s1` is the case that settles the argument for a second gate. It is a
154-residue prefix of a 157-residue training sequence, so the exact gate — which
compares whole strings — reports no match, while every residue of the target is
in training verbatim. This is exactly the embedded-target case the query-only
coverage rule exists to catch.

Note also that four exclusions match RCSB entries, not AFDB: the release cutoff
removes exact matches, never homologs. `2b5e` and `5t91` were admitted years
before 2020-05-01 and are still 40% and 39% homologs of CASP targets.

**Report coverage as 62/70, 19/22, and 18/21.** The full result, including the
MMseqs2 version, input hashes, and exact command, is in
`data/audit/homology_gate.json`.

CAMEO22 was screened separately against the same frozen training union and
thresholds. Its report is `data/audit/cameo22_homology_gate.json`; 115/183
targets have a qualifying full-chain hit, leaving 68/183. Unlike CASP, this
artifact has no frozen official evaluation-unit/domain segmentation. The 68
targets may therefore be called **full-chain homology-admitted**, but not fully
coordinate-training-clean under the stronger domain-local rule. Report the
full 183-target diagnostic and the 68-target subset side by side.

## Dataset roles

| Dataset | Role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CASP14 whole-chain (70) | primary comparison, 62 scoreable | outside the corpus under this contract; usable only while sampler settings are frozen against the post-cutoff pool and not against CASP14 |
| CASP15 strict single-chain (22) | primary comparison, 19 scoreable | 3 excluded by the homology screen |
| CASP16 strict single-chain (21) | primary comparison, 18 scoreable | 3 excluded by the homology screen |
| CAMEO22 (183) | secondary diagnostic, 68 full-chain admitted | 115 excluded by the full-chain homology screen; no frozen domain-local gate |
| RCSB post-cutoff pool | confirmatory selection set | every entry released after the cutoff; the only set that may be used to choose a checkpoint or sampler |

CASP14 is reportable here in a way it was not under the previous contract,
where six of its sequences matched the training corpus exactly and where the
geometry losses and guidance had been developed against it. Both conditions are
gone: this corpus excludes those sequences by date, and this track trains
without those loss terms. Eight further targets are excluded by homology, so the
reportable set is 62 rather than 70. The one carried-over hazard is sampler
choice, which is why sampler settings are frozen against the post-cutoff pool.

## Comparator rules

- SimpleFold-360M is the primary size-matched public baseline, and since the
  in-house transformer arm was cancelled it is now the **only** comparator. It
  is **not** a controlled ablation: it differs in PLM, corpus size, and training
  infrastructure, so the reportable claim is "a pure-SSM trunk trained on this
  corpus under this objective scores X against a published attention baseline",
  never "the mixer is what accounts for the difference". Any table or abstract
  that asserts the stronger form is wrong.
- The weaker claim is not nothing: the corpus, the objective, the crop and the
  benchmark protocol are all fixed by this project's own contract, so the number
  is reproducible here even though it is not attributable.
- Every comparison table must state the exposure asymmetry: this corpus is a
  strict subset of SimpleFold's, which additionally pretrains on AFESM.
- Report coverage before averages. Never define a common subset by a baseline's
  inference success.
- DPLM-2 all-atom lDDT is not comparable because its output omits full
  side-chain atoms; use backbone lDDT for the local-quality comparison.

## Required artifacts

For every scored set, preserve the original FASTA and its SHA-256; the training
FASTAs and their SHA-256 values; the exact-overlap JSON from
`pipeline/08_leakage_gates.py`; the MMseqs2 result table, version, database
hashes, and command; and the final admitted FASTA, excluded-target list,
checkpoint hash, sampler settings, seeds, and scorer version.
